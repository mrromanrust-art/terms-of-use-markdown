Processing of Data for AI Features

Linked Helper offers artificial intelligence-powered tools designed to
streamline sales outreach and profile analysis, including AI Messages, AI
Comments, and AI ICP Detection.

1. What Data Is Transferred

When you utilize AI features, the Application transmits specific contextual data
required to generate responses or perform evaluations. This may include:

•  Text content of target LinkedIn profiles (e.g., job title, headline, summary,
   work history, skills, and public posts);
•  Previous message history or interaction context relevant to the outreach
   sequence;
•  User-defined prompts, guidelines, or custom instructions;
•  Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) criteria and qualification rules defined in your
   campaign settings.

2. AI Service Providers (Sub-processors)

To process AI requests, we utilize enterprise API interfaces of specialized
third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI LLC). Data is transmitted securely via
encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS).

3. Purpose of Processing and AI Safety

•  Purpose: The data transferred is processed solely to generate natural,
   personalized message drafts, generate relevant post comments, or evaluate
   whether a target profile matches your specified ICP rules.
•  No Training on Your Data: Data submitted to third-party AI providers via
   enterprise APIs is strictly used to serve your real-time requests and is not
   used by us or our AI sub-processors to train or improve public foundation AI
   models.
•  User Control: You retain full control over whether AI features are enabled
   and can review or edit AI-generated content before or during campaign
   execution.

Workspaces and Team Data Access Model

Linked Helper provides a Workspaces architecture that enables businesses,
agencies, and teams to collaborate, manage licenses, and delegate outreach
workflows.

1. Role-Based Access Control & Visibility

To uphold data privacy and organizational security, data visibility within a
Workspace is governed by strict role-based access rules:

•  Workspace Owners and Admins: Maintain high-level administrative oversight
   over the entire organization. They can manage licenses, invite or remove team
   members, assign instances/accounts, and view aggregate workspace usage
   statistics and billing data.
•  Assigned Team Members & Contractors: Team members only have access to the
   specific LinkedIn accounts, instances, and campaigns explicitly assigned to
   them by an Owner or Admin.
•  Data Isolation: LinkedIn accounts, messages, lead databases, and campaign
   histories remain invisible to teammates unless explicitly granted permission.
   External contractors or specific team members cannot access unassigned
   accounts or data within the Workspace.

2. Shared Workspace Resources

Certain administrative assets—such as shared campaign templates, general
settings, or aggregated workspace reports—may be accessible to authorized team
members within the Workspace according to the permissions assigned by the
Workspace Owner or Administrator.
